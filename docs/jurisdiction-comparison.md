# Turnstile — Jurisdiction Comparison

*2026-10-08*

Where to incorporate a crypto payments company that receives USDC and pays out fiat — compared across Dubai, Estonia, and Switzerland.

## Side-by-side comparison

| | Dubai (VARA) | Estonia (MiCA CASP) | Switzerland (SRO / FINMA) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Regulator** | VARA (Dubai) | Finantsinspektsioon (FSA) | FINMA / SRO (e.g. VQF) |
| **License type** | VARA activity license | MiCA CASP authorization | SRO membership (light) or FINMA fintech license (heavy) |
| **Minimum capital** | AED 100K–4M ($27K–$1.1M) depending on activity | €50K–150K ($55K–$165K) depending on service class | CHF 15K–25K (SRO) or CHF 300K–1M (FINMA) |
| **Application fee** | AED 40K–100K ($11K–$27K) | €3,000 | Included in SRO setup; FINMA varies |
| **Annual supervision** | AED 80K–200K+ ($22K–$55K) | Included in FSA oversight | CHF 2K–5K (SRO annual) |
| **Staff requirements** | 2 Responsible Individuals (AED 200K–500K/yr each) | 2 board members, compliance officer, MLRO | Resident director, AML department |
| **Local presence** | UAE office required | Real office in Estonia | Swiss office required |
| **Total setup cost** | $100K–$300K+ | €150K–300K ($165K–$330K) | CHF 15K–25K (SRO) or CHF 100K–250K (FINMA) |
| **Timeline** | 6–12 months | ~3 months | 5–6 months (SRO) or 6–12 months (FINMA) |
| **Corporate tax** | 9% (above AED 375K profit) | 0% on retained profits; 20% on distribution | 12–14% (varies by canton) |
| **Personal income tax** | 0% | 20% flat | Progressive, up to ~40% |
| **Market access** | UAE + GCC | All 27 EU/EEA countries (MiCA passport) | Switzerland only (no EU passport) |
| **Banking access** | Easier for crypto companies | Improving but still cautious | Challenging; crypto-friendly banks exist (Sygnum, SEBA) |
| **Reputation** | Growing, crypto-forward | Solid EU jurisdiction | Highest ("Crypto Valley", Zug) |
| **Key risk** | Young regulator, rules still evolving | MiCA compliance is strict and new | SRO scope is limited; FINMA license is expensive |

Capital figures for Dubai depend on activity type. Transfer & Settlement (closest to Turnstile) requires AED 100K ($27K). Custody adds AED 4M ($1.1M). Requirements are cumulative if combining activities.

Estonia's old lightweight VASP regime ended July 2026. All new applicants go through MiCA, which is heavier but gives EU-wide passporting.

Switzerland's SRO route is the cheapest entry point across all three jurisdictions. It covers AML-regulated financial intermediary activities. A full FINMA license is needed for deposit-taking or larger-scale operations.

## Recommendation for Turnstile

**Start with Switzerland (SRO)** — CHF 15–25K to set up, 5–6 months, and the strongest reputation in crypto. The SRO route covers Turnstile's core activity (receiving USDC, verifying payments, proxying requests) under AML-regulated financial intermediary status without a full banking license.

**Upgrade path:** if Turnstile grows to hold significant client funds or operate the fiat off-ramp directly, apply for a FINMA fintech license or the upcoming FINMA Crypto Institution license (proposed October 2025, expected 2026–2027).

**If EU-wide access matters more than cost,** Estonia is the pick. One MiCA CASP license covers all 27 EU countries, but the setup cost is 5–10x higher than Switzerland SRO and the compliance burden is heavier.

**Dubai** makes sense primarily for the 0% personal income tax. The VARA license is expensive (mandatory Responsible Individuals at $55–136K/yr each) and the regulatory framework is still maturing.
